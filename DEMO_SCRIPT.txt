# CampaignPulse — Demo Script (3 minutes)
# Just the live demo. Record screen + narrate.

================================================================================
## INTRO (15 seconds)
================================================================================

[SCREEN: CampaignPulse app open — show the sidebar with 3 phases listed]

"This is CampaignPulse — an AI pre-launch campaign intelligence tool with three 
phases: Brief Scoring, Plan Generation, and Alignment Audit. Let me walk through 
a live demo."


================================================================================
## PHASE 1: BRIEF SCORING (70 seconds)
================================================================================

[SCREEN: Click "HP Enterprise MFP (Q3)" in the sidebar under Sample Briefs]

"I'm loading an HP Enterprise MFP campaign brief — this targets 4,500 managed 
print service contracts in Q3. 286 words."

[SCREEN: Click "Score Brief" — wait for results]

"Scoring it now."

[SCREEN: Results appear — show the top metrics row]

"Overall score: 61 out of 100. Readiness tier: Needs Refinement. 4 gaps found, 
and it estimates 14 hours of rework if we proceed without fixing them. Analysis 
took about 16 seconds."

[SCREEN: Scroll to Dimension Breakdown]

"Let's look at the six dimensions:

Audience Precision — 84. Strong. It defines buyer titles, company size, priority 
verticals, fleet size signals. Good for targeting.

Channel Specificity — 48. Channels are named but too broad. No platforms, no ad 
formats, no placements, no ownership by channel.

Message-Product Fit — 90. Excellent. Tight connection to product differentiators 
— lower cost-per-page, HP Wolf Security, Microsoft 365 integration.

Budget Allocation — zero. No budget mentioned anywhere. This is a critical 
execution blocker.

Timeline Feasibility — 35. It just says 'Q3.' No launch date, no asset 
deadlines, no QA windows.

Measurement Hookability — 72. Has outcome KPIs and targets, but attribution 
rules and channel-level tracking aren't defined."

[SCREEN: Show Top Issues section]

"The top issues are clear: no budget, channels too generic, timeline too vague."

[SCREEN: Show Clarifying Questions at the bottom]

"And here's what makes this useful — it generates specific questions to resolve 
each gap. 'What is the total campaign budget and how should it split across 
channels?' 'What are the exact launch dates and QA windows?' These are the 
questions a senior campaign manager would ask in a kickoff meeting — generated 
instantly, before any meeting happens."


================================================================================
## PHASE 2: PLAN GENERATION (45 seconds)
================================================================================

[SCREEN: Navigate to Phase 2 — show "Brief loaded" indicator]

"Moving to Phase 2. The brief carries over automatically. I'll provide context 
for the gaps — budget is 1.2 million, social means LinkedIn Sponsored Content 
and Twitter Ads, launch date is July 7th."

[SCREEN: Type answers in the additional instructions box, click "Generate 
Execution Plan"]

"Generating the plan."

[SCREEN: Show Channels tab]

"It produces a full channel-by-channel execution plan. Each channel has: 
platform details, audience targeting, ad format, budget allocation, CTA, copy 
guidance, and an owner."

[SCREEN: Click Timeline tab]

"Week-by-week timeline with milestones and QA windows."

[SCREEN: Click Tracking tab]

"Tracking setup with UTM structure, KPIs per channel, and reporting cadence. 
This is operational — an agency can execute from this without a kickoff meeting."


================================================================================
## PHASE 3: ALIGNMENT AUDIT (60 seconds)
================================================================================

[SCREEN: Navigate to Phase 3. Check "Load sample assets" → select "Microsoft 
Surface Pro 11". Load the Surface brief from sidebar.]

"Phase 3 — alignment audit. I'm loading a Microsoft Surface Pro brief and four 
sample assets: email draft, LinkedIn ad, sales outreach, and landing page. Some 
have deliberate misalignments."

[SCREEN: Click "Run Alignment Audit"]

"Running the audit."

[SCREEN: Show Semantic Scores tab]

"First — embedding-based semantic similarity. Each asset gets a percentage score 
for how closely it aligns with the brief at the language level."

[SCREEN: Click Issues tab — show critical findings]

"Now the issues:

Critical — the email mentions iPad by name. The brief explicitly says 'do not 
reference Apple or iPad.' Brand violation.

Critical — the LinkedIn ad targets startups and freelancers. The brief targets 
IT Directors at 500-plus employee enterprises. Audience mismatch.

Warning — the email CTA says 'Learn More.' The brief objective is 'Book a 
Demo.' CTA drift."

[SCREEN: Show Channel Scores tab — show passing assets]

"But sales outreach and landing page pass clean. The system doesn't false-flag 
— it distinguishes real errors from intentional channel adaptations."


================================================================================
## CLOSE (10 seconds)
================================================================================

[SCREEN: Show the app homepage]

"That's CampaignPulse. Score the brief, generate the plan, audit before launch 
— before you spend a dollar."

[END]
