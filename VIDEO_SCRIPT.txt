================================================================================

[SCREEN: Title slide or CampaignPulse homepage]

VOICEOVER:
"In enterprise B2B marketing, every campaign begins with a brief — a document 
that defines who you're targeting, what channels to use, the budget, timeline, 
and success metrics. But here's the gap nobody talks about: there is zero 
quality control between writing the brief and spending the money.

The result? Ambiguous briefs cause 8 to 15 hours of rework per campaign. Channel

cross-checks them systematically. And misaligned assets underperform by 20 to 35 
percent. The worst part? Nobody catches it until post-campaign analysis — when 
the budget is already spent.

This is the problem I chose to solve: the pre-launch intelligence gap. How do 
you catch execution failures before they happen?"

## SECTION 5: TECHNOLOGY STACK (30 seconds)
================================================================================

[SCREEN: Show architecture diagram or code structure]

VOICEOVER:
"Let me walk through the tech stack.

The frontend is built with Streamlit for rapid prototyping with a professional UI.

The intelligence layer uses Azure OpenAI — specifically GPT for structured 
reasoning and text-embedding-3-small for semantic similarity computation. 

The architecture is multi-agent: three specialized agents — Brief Scorer, Plan 
Generator, and Alignment Auditor — each with their own prompt engineering and 
output schemas. The agents use structured JSON output for reliability.

The semantic audit in Phase 3 combines LLM-based reasoning with cosine 
similarity on embeddings — giving both qualitative and quantitative alignment 
scores. This dual approach reduces hallucination and makes the system 
trustworthy."
