CampaignPulse: AI Pre-Launch Campaign Intelligence

Enterprise B2B technology companies run 50+ marketing campaigns per quarter, each beginning with a campaign brief that defines audience, channels, budget, timeline, and success metrics. However, no systematic quality gate exists between brief creation and campaign execution. Industry research shows that ambiguous briefs cause 8-15 hours of rework per campaign, misaligned channel assets underperform by 20-35%, and execution failures are only detected during post-campaign analysis — after the budget is spent.

CampaignPulse addresses this gap with a three-phase AI-powered pre-launch intelligence system:

Phase 1 — Brief Readiness Scoring: A specialized agent evaluates campaign briefs across six weighted dimensions (Audience Precision, Channel Specificity, Message-Product Fit, Budget Allocation, Timeline Feasibility, and Measurement Hookability), producing a 0-100 composite score with dimension-level justifications and targeted clarifying questions to resolve gaps before execution begins.

Phase 2 — Execution Plan Generation: Using the scored brief and gap resolutions as input, a second agent generates a structured, channel-level execution plan including platform-specific targeting parameters, copy guidance, weekly timelines, budget allocations, and tracking setup with UTM structures and KPIs.

Phase 3 — Pre-Launch Alignment Audit: A third agent audits every channel asset (emails, ads, landing pages) against the original brief using a dual approach: LLM-based reasoning for qualitative misalignment detection and embedding-based cosine similarity for quantitative semantic scoring. This surfaces brand violations, audience mismatches, CTA drift, and messaging inconsistencies before launch.

The system is built on Azure OpenAI (GPT for structured reasoning, text-embedding-3-small for semantic similarity), Streamlit for the interface, and a multi-agent architecture with JSON-structured outputs. CampaignPulse transforms pre-launch review from subjective gut-feel coordination into a scored, audited, data-informed process — catching execution failures before they cost budget.
